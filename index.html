Notes are shown as sounding pitch in equal temperament. Tenor parts are usually written an octave higher than they sound, so a written C4 appears here as C3. Use headphones if you sing along to a backing track, otherwise the track will be picked up as well. Raise sensitivity if quiet notes are missed, lower it if background noise triggers the trace.
